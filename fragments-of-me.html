Fragments of Me

A POETRY COLLECTION BY BK FUTURE

Fragments of Me gathers the poems into one collection—a place for midnight thoughts, unspoken feelings, identity, love, loss, healing, darkness, and the small moments that leave a permanent mark.

Each poem is a fragment; together, they form a record of everything words helped hold.
