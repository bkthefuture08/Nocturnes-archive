MANUSCRIPT 007

A Dove and a Raven

FICTION • ROMANCE • OPPOSITES ATTRACT FRIENDS TO LOVERS • DESTINED LOVE

He was raised to be a dove.

She was raised to be a raven.

Allan Joseph is nineteen, the pastor's son, and everything his parents expect him to be: kind, obedient, faithful, and good. But behind the sermons and perfect answers, Allan has questions he's never been brave enough to ask.

Rune Roth is eighteen, a goth girl who would rather be left alone than explain herself. Born and raised in South Africa before moving to Los Angeles, she carries a family legacy she wants nothing to do with—and beliefs she's no longer sure belong to her anymore.

They were never supposed to meet.

A Christian pastor's son.

A girl raised in a very different faith.

Two people who have every reason to walk away from each other.

But sometimes, the person who challenges everything you believe is the person who teaches you the most about yourself.

Maybe Allan isn't as certain as he thought.

Maybe Rune isn't as lost as everyone assumes.

And maybe a dove and a raven aren't as different as they seem.
