SOUNDTRACK 001

Shameless

Camila Cabello

DARK POP • OBSESSION • DESIRE

There is a certain kind of desire that becomes impossible to hide.

Shameless belongs to that moment—the point where restraint starts to crumble and pretending not to care becomes more exhausting than admitting that you do.

It carries the feeling of wanting something with your entire being, even when the consequences are complicated.

A song for secret glances, dangerous honesty, midnight thoughts, and the moment when someone finally stops pretending they don't want what they want.

Some feelings don't knock.

They simply walk through the door.

SOUNDTRACK 002

I See Red

Everybody Loves an Outlaw

DARK COUNTRY • OBSESSION • RETRIBUTION

Not every love story stays soft.

I See Red lives in the darker territory where affection, jealousy and anger begin to blur together.

It feels like the soundtrack to a character standing on the edge of losing control—not because they stopped caring, but because their emotions have become impossible to ignore.

The atmosphere is raw, possessive and unsettling.

It belongs to the side of Nocturne's Archive where love isn't always beautiful, and emotions don't always arrive quietly.

Sometimes the heart doesn't break.

Sometimes it burns.

SOUNDTRACK 003

Renegade

Aaryan Shah

DARK POP • REBELLION • INTENSITY

Renegade feels like stepping outside the lines and refusing to apologise for what you find there.

There is darkness in it, but also freedom—the feeling of becoming someone unpredictable, untouchable and impossible to fit neatly into the expectations of everyone around you.

It belongs to characters who don't necessarily want to be saved.

Characters who walk toward the forbidden door simply because someone told them not to open it.

A soundtrack for rebellion, temptation, dangerous confidence, and the beautiful chaos of becoming your own kind of villain.
