MANUSCRIPT 001

Shades of Obsidian

MAFIA • DARK FICTION

She has spent years trying to bury the secrets that came with her past, hiding every skeleton behind the carefully pressed uniform of an ordinary life. For a while, it works. Until a certain Mafia heir walks into her world. What begins as an unexpected intrusion threatens to unravel the life she's worked so hard to construct, forcing old secrets back into the light and bringing her dangerously close to a world she thought she'd left behind. Because some secrets refuse to stay buried. And some people have a way of finding exactly what you're trying to hide.

MANUSCRIPT 002

I Belong to You

MAFIA • ROMANCE • DRAMA

She had a normal life. At least, she thought she did. Unlike her cousin, whose life had already become entangled with a dangerous world, she had managed to remain far from it—living quietly, loving quietly, and believing that her future belonged entirely to her. Until a Mafia leader walks into her life. Powerful. Ruthless. Untouchable. And far too interested in her. He belongs to a world she was never meant to enter, while she is the last person he should ever want. Yet somehow, their paths become impossible to separate. Because when a man accustomed to possessing everything decides that she belongs to him, walking away may no longer be an option.

MANUSCRIPT 003

The Mafia Beside Me

MAFIA • MYSTERY • DRAMA

She was the girl who found a family when she needed one most. Welcomed into the home of three siblings after growing up in foster care, she slowly became part of something she had never truly believed she could have—a family. The youngest becomes her responsibility. The oldest becomes something far more complicated. A brother. A best friend. A secret she refuses to name. But lately, the oldest has become increasingly distant. Always busy. Always somewhere else. Always unavailable whenever she needs him most. She tells herself there is a reasonable explanation. There isn't. When she finally discovers where he's been—and what he's been involved in—the truth is far darker than anything she could have imagined. And suddenly, the person she thought was simply standing beside her... has been living an entirely different life.

MANUSCRIPT 004

What Are We?

GOTH • PUNK • DRAMA

In their world, Goths and Punks don't mix. The rules are simple. The lines are drawn. And if you're a Goth, you stay with the Goths. She has spent years trying to earn her place among them. As a Raven chick, she's considered too inexperienced to be part of the inner circle—a position that comes with respect, belonging, and a voice within the Goth community. But she's determined to prove that she's more than the label they've given her. Getting accepted into the Goth club would mean finally being seen as one of them. So she's willing to do whatever it takes. Then she meets him. A Punk. The one person she knows she shouldn't be drawn to. What begins as a complicated relationship quickly becomes something neither of them can neatly define. Because the more she tries to figure out what they are, the more complicated everything becomes. Especially when she discovers that he's already tangled up with someone else. Someone she knows. Her best friend. A Goth just like her. In a world where choosing sides matters, falling for the wrong person could cost her the very acceptance she's spent years chasing. So what are they? Friends? A mistake? Something more? Or just another secret waiting to tear the whole circle apart?

MANUSCRIPT 005

Be Mine

DRAMA • MYSTERY • ROMANCE

They've been friends for as long as either of them can remember. Two people who grew up side by side, carrying completely different kinds of pain. She lives with an everlasting grief she has never quite learned how to put down. He lives with the guilt of knowing he is somehow connected to it. She has hopes for the future. A life she still wants to build despite everything she's lost. He has secrets. Secrets buried deep enough that he convinces himself they can stay there forever. Secrets she was never supposed to discover. But secrets have a way of surfacing. And whatever is done in the dark will eventually come into the light. As the years of friendship begin to collide with feelings neither of them can ignore, the line between what they know about each other and what they've been hiding becomes dangerously thin. Then one secret changes everything. With every revelation comes another question. Every answer opens another wound. And the closer they get to the truth, the more they risk destroying the very bond that has kept them together for years. Because sometimes the person who knows you best is also the person who has the most to hide. And when the darkness finally comes to light... will there be anything left to hold onto? Be Mine.

MANUSCRIPT 006

(Just) Best Friends Forever

FRIENDS TO LOVERS • DRAMA • FANTASY

They were supposed to be best friends forever. Nothing more. At least, that's what they told themselves. They grew up together, shared everything, and somehow always found their way back to each other—even when life gave them every reason not to. But forever has never been as simple as it sounds. There are exes who refuse to stay in the past, accidents that change everything, regrets that cannot be undone, and tragedies that leave scars neither of them knows how to carry. Somewhere along the way, friendship becomes something neither of them can name without risking everything they've built. And just when they think they've finally figured out where they stand, life pulls the ground from beneath them again. Because sometimes love arrives too early. Sometimes it arrives too late. And sometimes, no matter how many times the world tries to separate two people, they keep finding their way back to one another. Across distance. Across time. Across worlds. Because some connections were never meant to end. And maybe being best friends forever was never supposed to mean they would remain just friends.

SOUND RECORD 001

Shameless

CAMILA CABELLO • DARK POP • OBSESSION • DESIRE

There is a certain kind of desire that becomes impossible to hide. Shameless belongs to that moment—the point where restraint starts to crumble and pretending not to care becomes more exhausting than admitting that you do. It carries the feeling of wanting something with your entire being, even when the consequences are complicated. A song for secret glances, dangerous honesty, midnight thoughts, and the moment when someone finally stops pretending they don't want what they want. Some feelings don't knock. They simply walk through the door.

SOUND RECORD 002

I See Red

EVERYBODY LOVES AN OUTLAW • DARK COUNTRY • OBSESSION • RETRIBUTION

Not every love story stays soft. I See Red lives in the darker territory where affection, jealousy and anger begin to blur together. It feels like the soundtrack to a character standing on the edge of losing control—not because they stopped caring, but because their emotions have become impossible to ignore. The atmosphere is raw, possessive and unsettling. It belongs to the side of Nocturne's Archive where love isn't always beautiful, and emotions don't always arrive quietly. Sometimes the heart doesn't break. Sometimes it burns.

SOUND RECORD 003

Renegade

AARYAN SHAH • DARK POP • REBELLION • INTENSITY

Renegade feels like stepping outside the lines and refusing to apologise for what you find there. There is darkness in it, but also freedom—the feeling of becoming someone unpredictable, untouchable and impossible to fit neatly into the expectations of everyone around you. It belongs to characters who don't necessarily want to be saved. Characters who walk toward the forbidden door simply because someone told them not to open it. A soundtrack for rebellion, temptation, dangerous confidence, and the beautiful chaos of becoming your own kind of villain.
